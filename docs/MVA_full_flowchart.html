outside the scripts

Phase: parental testing or long reads.

Finally, a clinical scientist assigns an ACMG classification to each confirmed allele.

Known limits of step 4

No REVEL / SpliceAI / CADD, so missense and splice evidence is weaker.

Pairs of hits outside one phase block, or SV + small variant, stay unphased until confirmed.

The BAM chromosome screen only detects one chromosome changed in more than ~15% of cells, so a normal result does not rule out MVA.

Partly functional second alleles above 1% frequency are not yet considered.
